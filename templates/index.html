Phase 1 — Business Understanding · CRISP-DM
General and Specific Objectives

Analyze video game player behavior and its relationship with mental health through the CRISP-DM methodology and data mining techniques, identifying gaming and lifestyle patterns that influence addiction risk and psychological well-being dimensions.

01
Specific Objective 1
Examine the dataset under the CRISP-DM methodology
  • A higher number of daily gaming hours daily_gaming_hours is associated with a higher addiction risk level
  • The presence of withdrawal symptoms withdrawal_symptoms increases the probability of high-risk classification
  • A lower number of sleep hours sleep_hours reflects greater habit intensity and higher risk
  • A high social isolation score social_isolation_score predicts high-risk player profiles
✅ Objective achieved — Random Forest 65% accuracy · Correlation > 0.60
02
Specific Objective 2
Segment players according to physical and mental health impact
  • Players with higher daily_gaming_hours and lower sleep_quality show higher social_isolation_score
  • Players with lower daily_gaming_hours and higher exercise_hours_weekly show higher face_to_face_social_hours_weekly
  • Older players with more years_gaming show differentiated monthly_game_spending_usd
  • Players with higher daily_gaming_hours show higher weight_change_kg and mood_swing_frequency
✅ Objective achieved — K-Means k=4 · 4 differentiated profiles
03
Specific Objective 3
Build a regression model to estimate risk level
  • daily_gaming_hours and withdrawal_symptoms are the strongest predictors
  • The combination of loss_of_other_interests and continued_despite_problems improves predictive capability
  • Low sleep_quality combined with high social_isolation_score contributes to estimating medium and high-risk levels
  • The model achieves acceptable performance measured with R², MAE, and RMSE
✅ Objective achieved — R²=0.9429 · MAE=0.2195 · RMSE=0.2709
